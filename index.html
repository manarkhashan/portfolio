About Me

I’m a 24-year-old creator who lives at the intersection of stories, art, and logic. I’m a native speaker of three languages (English, Arabic, and Hebrew) and I’m currently challenging myself to learn a fourth: Dutch. I’m hovering at an A1/A2 level, but I’m getting there!

At my core, I’m a storyteller. I’m a self-published author with a 5.0-star poetry book and a 70,000-word fantasy novel currently in the works. For me, a story isn't just words on a page. It’s an experience. I’ve spent the last few years learning how to take those narratives and turn them into something interactive. Whether that’s through pixel art, 2D animation, or coding in C# and Unity 6.

I recently put all these pieces together to build my first game on Itch.io. I handled the writing, the branching paths, the art, and the code. It was a massive learning curve, but working with friends to test, break, and fix the game taught me more about "real-world" development than any tutorial ever could.

I built this portfolio from scratch to show you not just what I can do, but how I think. I believe in creating work that people actually connect with. And seeing my digital stories reach 20,000+ readers with a 100% positive sentiment makes me even more excited for whatever I build next.

Scattered : The Game

5.0 Star rated tell-tale game.

Scattered is a surreal, choice driven story told through falling dreams, shifting worlds, and strange creatures.

You wake up in the middle of the sky, no memory, no direction. Every choice drops you deeper into a world stitched together by caves, witches, and talking animals. The story changes as you wander, twisting between wonder and dread.

This is an interactive narrative built in Twine, featuring short looping animations and multiple branching paths. It’s quiet, weird, sometimes funny, sometimes uncomfortable.

like a dream that’s trying to tell you something you can’t quite understand.
